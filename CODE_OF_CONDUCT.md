# Code of conduct

This page says what is expected of everyone who takes part in Parasma's
issues, pull requests and discussions, and what happens when someone falls
short.

## Expected

- Be civil and assume good faith. Most people asking a question have read
  less of the code than you have, and most people answering have less time
  than you would like.
- Keep criticism on the work. "This breaks the layering rule" is useful;
  remarks about the person who wrote it are not.
- Respect other people's documents. Never post, quote from or name a document
  that is not yours to share (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity
  or background.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after it has been decided.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request.
Reports reach the maintainers and are not shown publicly.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what crosses the line and may edit or delete content,
lock a thread, or block an account from the repository. Small lapses usually
get a private word first; serious or repeated ones do not.
